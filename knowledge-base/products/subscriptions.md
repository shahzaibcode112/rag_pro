# Brewly Subscriptions

Customers can subscribe to receive coffee every 2, 4, or 6 weeks.
Subscribers get 10% off every order and free shipping on all subscription deliveries.

You can pause, skip, or cancel a subscription at any time from your account page.
Changes must be made at least 3 days before your next shipment date.
The "Explorer" subscription sends a different single-origin coffee each month for $19 per bag.
