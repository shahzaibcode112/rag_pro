# About Brewly Coffee

Brewly Coffee is a specialty coffee roaster founded in 2018 in Portland, Oregon by Maya Torres and Daniel Kim.
We source beans directly from small farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, and roast every batch in small lots.

Our mission is to make fresh, ethically sourced coffee easy to enjoy at home.
We have 42 employees and ship to customers across the United States and Canada.
Our headquarters and roastery are located at 218 Alder Street, Portland, Oregon.
