# Shipping and Returns

## Shipping
Standard shipping costs $5 and takes 3-5 business days. Orders over $40 ship free.
Express shipping costs $12 and takes 1-2 business days.
We ship to the US and Canada. Canadian orders may take an additional 3-5 business days.

## Returns
Because coffee is a perishable food product, we do not accept returns of opened bags.
If you are unhappy with your coffee, contact support within 14 days of delivery and we will offer a replacement or a refund.
Damaged or incorrect orders are always replaced free of charge.
